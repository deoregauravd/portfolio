Core Privacy Principle: Offline-First & Zero Tracking

ProductivityHub ("the App"), developed by independent developer Gaurav Deore (operating under DevPhoton), is built with a strict privacy-first, offline-first philosophy.

Summary: Your time blocks, focus sessions, distraction records ("minus minutes"), tasks, habits, and notes stay strictly on your device. We do not collect, sell, lease, or broker your personal information. There are no advertising trackers, behavioral telemetry, or third-party user analytics inside the app.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Account Required: You can download and use all primary features without creating an account, registering an email address, or logging in.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not harvest names, telephone numbers, contact lists, photos, or GPS location coordinates.
  • No Analytics SDKs: The App does not contain third-party tracking libraries (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, Facebook SDK, or AppsFlyer).
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

2. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All application data is written exclusively to the local, private sandbox storage of your Android device:

  • Timeline Logs & Hourly Blocks: Categorized hourly entries, activities, notes, hurdle logs, and flow state flags.
  • Distraction Tracking: "Minus minutes" deductions and distraction reason labels.
  • Habit Records: Daily and weekly completion states, streaks, and habits configuration.
  • Markdown Notes & Activity Templates: Custom 1-tap template chips and structured markdown personal notes.
  • Local AES Encryption (Vault): If enabled, your sensitive notes and logs are encrypted locally with hardware-backed AES-256 encryption. Encryption keys remain solely within your device's secure enclave and are never accessible to the developer.

3. Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

ProductivityHub requests only specific Android system permissions necessary to provide optional convenience and security features:

  • Biometric Sensors (USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT): Used solely through the standard Android BiometricPrompt system framework to authenticate you when unlocking the app or opening your encrypted vault. Biometric data is processed directly by the device hardware security module (TEE/StrongBox); the app never accesses, logs, or transmits raw fingerprint or facial data.
  • Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO): An optional permission utilized strictly when you explicitly activate voice actions or the live voice assistant. Speech is converted to text either directly on-device or routed through your configured AI endpoint for productivity commands. Audio is never recorded in the background or stored remotely.
  • Calendar Access (READ_CALENDAR / WRITE_CALENDAR): Optional permission requested only if you choose to synchronize or export your hourly schedule blocks to your device's calendar. Calendar data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted to any third party.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used exclusively for optional, user-directed features:
    • AI Assistant (Gemini API): If you configure an AI API key or endpoint to generate daily summaries or productivity advice, prompts and productivity contexts are sent directly between your device and the AI provider (e.g., Google Gemini).
    • Cloud Backup (Google Drive Sync): If you enable Google Drive backup, your encrypted data archive is uploaded directly to your personal Google Drive storage via official Google APIs. The developer operates no intermediary server.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services: App distribution, app updates, and Android shortcut integrations are provided by Google Play under the official Google Privacy Policy.
  • Optional AI Providers (e.g., Google Gemini): When you enable and use AI capabilities with your own API key, queries are processed in accordance with Google's API Terms and Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive (Optional): If you choose to sync backups to Google Drive, data is handled by your personal Google account.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion

  • Data Portability: You can export your timeline data and history at any time to standard CSV or JSON format for external backup or analysis.
  • Data Deletion: Because all data resides locally on your device, you have complete control over its retention. You can permanently delete all app data at any time by navigating to:
    Settings > Apps > ProductivityHub > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data
    or by simply uninstalling the application.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

ProductivityHub is designed for general audiences and productivity tracking. Because the application does not collect personally identifiable information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with a revised "Effective Date" timestamp.
