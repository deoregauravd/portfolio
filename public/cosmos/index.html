Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cosmos Explorer ("the App"), developed independently by sole developer Gaurav Deore (operating under the developer moniker DevPhoton), handles data on Android devices.

As a sole developer, user privacy is respected by design. By downloading, installing, or using the App, the terms described in this policy apply.

1. Information Collection and Handling

No personal data or personally identifiable information (PII) is ever collected, stored, transmitted, or sold.

  • No Account Required: Registration, account creation, or login is not required to explore the cosmos.
  • No Personal Identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, photos, or precise GPS location coordinates are not accessed or harvested.
  • No Telemetry or Behavioral Tracking: The App contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, user tracking modules, or diagnostic telemetry.

2. Android Hardware Permissions and On-Device Data

Cosmos Explorer requests specific standard Android permissions solely to deliver core interactive simulation features locally on the device:

  • Motion Sensors (Gyroscope & Accelerometer): Used exclusively in real-time volatile memory (RAM) to provide responsive tilt-reactive parallax effects and interactive camera movements when viewing celestial bodies. Sensor readings are never recorded, logged, or transmitted.
  • Wallpaper Service (SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS): If chosen to enable the optional live wallpaper feature, this permission allows the device to render the real-time 3D space scene as the home and lock screen wallpaper.
  • Local Device Storage: Simulation speed presets, visual scale settings, camera bookmarks, narrative space story progress (such as expedition logs), and graphics quality preferences are stored locally in the device's isolated app sandbox (via Unity PlayerPrefs and local JSON files). This data remains 100% on the device and is deleted when app data is cleared or uninstalled.
  • Google Play In-App Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): If optional celestial content or expeditions are unlocked, transactions are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing system. The developer never receives, accesses, or stores payment details or financial credentials.

3. Third-Party Services and Scientific Links

  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery, digital licensing, and in-app transactions are governed by Google Play. Google handles diagnostic metrics under the official Google Privacy Policy.
  • Educational & Scientific Citations: Celestial body info cards and mission catalogs provide optional hyperlinks to verified educational resources (such as NASA, JPL, and public scientific catalogs). Tapping an external link opens the device's default web browser, governed by that external site's policy.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Cosmos Explorer is designed as an inspiring, educational space simulation suitable for general audiences of all ages. Personal information is never collected from any user, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), fully complying with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families policy requirements.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Because all progress, unlocked stories, and camera presets are stored strictly on the local device, no user data exists on external servers. All locally stored data can be permanently erased at any time by clearing application storage via Android Settings > Apps > Cosmos Explorer > Storage > Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to maintain platform compliance. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
