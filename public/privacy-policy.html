Introduction

DevPhoton ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Cosmic Eye Live Interactive 3D Wallpaper ("the App") handles information on Android devices and supported platforms.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

  • No User Account: You can use all features of the App without registering, logging in, or providing any personal details.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, photos, or precise location coordinates.

2. Device Permissions and On-Device Data

The App requests standard Android permissions strictly to provide core features locally on your device:

  • Wallpaper Service (SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS): Used solely to render and display the real-time 3D simulation as your home and lock screen wallpaper.
  • Sensors (Gyroscope & Accelerometer): Used solely in real-time volatile memory to enable tilt-reactive parallax effects and camera movement. Sensor inputs are processed instantaneously and are never recorded or transmitted.
  • Local Storage: Your graphic settings, simulation speeds, camera bookmarks, and story saves are stored locally in isolated app sandbox storage (using Unity PlayerPrefs and local JSON files). This data never leaves your device.
  • Google Play Billing (BILLING): If you choose to make an optional in-app purchase, the transaction is processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or financial details.

3. Third-Party Services and Links

  • Google Play Services: Core distribution and purchase validation are handled via Google Play. Google may collect anonymized diagnostic and device metrics under their own Privacy Policy.
  • External Links: Educational sections (such as celestial body fact sheets or attribution links) may offer optional links to third-party scientific websites (e.g., NASA, Wikipedia). Tapping these links opens your device's web browser, which is governed by the destination site's privacy terms.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The application does not collect personal data from any user, fully complying with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families policies.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not collect or store user data on any remote servers, we do not maintain personal user records. All local app preferences and progress can be deleted at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app from your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
