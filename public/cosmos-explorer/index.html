Introduction

DevPhoton ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Cosmos Explorer ("the App"), an interactive 3D solar system simulation and space exploration application on Android, handles information.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the privacy practices outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to explore the cosmos.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, photos, or precise GPS location coordinates.
  • No Tracking or Telemetry: The App does not contain third-party behavioral analytics, user tracking SDKs, or background telemetry.

2. Android Hardware Permissions and On-Device Data

Cosmos Explorer requests specific standard Android permissions strictly to deliver core interactive simulation features locally on your device:

  • Motion Sensors (Gyroscope & Accelerometer): Used exclusively in real-time volatile memory (RAM) to provide responsive tilt-reactive parallax effects and interactive camera movements when viewing celestial bodies. Sensor readings are never recorded, logged, or transmitted off your device.
  • Wallpaper Service (SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS): If you choose to enable the live wallpaper feature, this permission allows your device to render the interactive 3D space scene as your home and lock screen wallpaper.
  • Local Device Storage: Simulation speed presets, visual scale settings, camera bookmarks, narrative story progress (such as space expedition logs), and graphics quality preferences are stored locally in your device's isolated app sandbox (using standard Unity PlayerPrefs and local JSON files). This data remains 100% on your device and is erased when you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Google Play In-App Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): If you choose to unlock optional celestial content or expeditions, purchases are handled directly through the official Google Play Billing system. DevPhoton never receives, accesses, or stores your payment details or credit card information.

3. Third-Party Services and Scientific Links

  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery, digital licensing, and in-app transactions are governed by Google Play. Google may collect anonymized diagnostic and device metrics under their own Google Privacy Policy.
  • Educational & Scientific Citations: Celestial body info cards and mission catalogs may include optional hyperlinks to verified educational and scientific resources (such as NASA, JPL, or public scientific catalogs). Tapping an external link opens your device's default web browser and is subject to the external website's privacy policy.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Cosmos Explorer is designed as an inspiring, educational space simulation suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the App collects zero personal data, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and Google Play Families policy requirements.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Because all progress, unlocked stories, and camera presets are stored strictly on your local device, we hold no user data on external cloud servers. You can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings > Apps > Cosmos Explorer > Storage > Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform compliance updates. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
